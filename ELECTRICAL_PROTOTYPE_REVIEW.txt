PROTOTYPE ELECTRICAL REVIEW — v0.0.39

Corrected design candidate for a small, current-limited bench prototype batch.
This is not production/USB-compliance or physical thermal qualification.

1. Display/MCU shared logic supply is3.23394V nominal using AP63200WU-7,
C2071868, with49.9k +300ohm upper divider and16.5k lower divider. Precision
49.9k/16.5k parts are0.1%,25ppm/C;300ohm trim is1%,100ppm/C. Static supply
bound including65C resistor temperature allowance is3.18878–3.27942V.
The prototype review reserves20mV positive ripple/overshoot below LCD3.30V.
The warmed0.6mm logic feeder,0.5A load allowance,5mV downstream drop and
10mV negative ripple allowance leave RP2350 USB supply>=3.1469V; the20mA
analog-load allowance and warm ferrite DCR leave VREG_AVDD>=3.1447V,
above the manufacturer3.135V minimum. These load/ripple allowances require
bench verification; they are not measured limits or hard current clamps.

2. R_VREG_AVDD now denotes a BLM15PD121SN1D ferrite/C76891, replacing
33ohm. The reference is retained so existing net/pin checks remain explicit.
A0.6mm bottom copper feed connects the actual converter output to the logic
rail; it does not use the feedback pin as a power tap. The AP63200 uses the
shared Diodes TSOT26 land pattern in its canonical pin1-right/bottom orientation:
PCB/CPL rotation0, supplier CAD world rotation180. Verify factory CPL preview.
The3.9uH output inductor remains within the adjustable-regulator ripple-current
selection equations for the5V input,500kHz design. Input10uF and2x22uF output
follow the manufacturer's nominal capacitor table. Effective DC-biased
capacitance, startup, PFM ripple, transient response and ferrite ringing need scope checks.

3. R_SPK_LIMIT is330kohm/C25778 in series after the coupling capacitor and
before PAM8403 INL. It lowers gain without a firmware-only volume limiter.
The conservative continuous-AC calculation, with internal-gain/component
margins, is0.707W into4ohm, or0.353W into the specified8ohm1W speaker.
Turn-on/off pops and actual speaker waveforms still require bench checks.
Neither speaker wire may be grounded. Maximum volume will be lower.

4. Battery inductor is APV APH0420T1R5M/C5349662,1.5uH, approximately
USD0.10. The1.5A battery-converter-output design target produces3.049A
estimated peak at3.2V input with conservative L/f/efficiency assumptions;
20% margin is3.659A, below4.57A lower tabulated saturation. No hardware
1.5A output clamp is added. Verify actual low-battery load and thermal behavior;
this does not qualify unrestricted overload or continuous2A boost operation.
